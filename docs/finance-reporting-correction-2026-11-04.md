Securities accounting is independent from living income and expenses by the owner's explicit request. Brokerage realized gains/losses, dividends, repo interest and fees remain auditable as separate facts. Bank/broker transfers remain asset movements; withdrawals may include realized profits. Floating gains and market values are not inferred from realized trades. Only complete owner-confirmed history with no remaining positions can make portfolio value equal confirmed cash.

A monthly net-income figure mixed realized securities losses with ordinary receipts, while an original WeChat teaching receipt marked incoming and deposited to change was treated as internal. Detailed monetary evidence remains in ignored local diagnostics. Source-confirmed external WeChat receipts now count as income; owner decisions, self/family transfers, settlement accounts, loans, refunds and ambiguous pass-through purposes stay protected. Classification never creates tuition payments.

Cloud-owned balance groups replace the asset/debt net subtotal: savings cards, card debt, mortgage principal, brokerage assets and other funds. Unknowns, billed-only debt, estimates, currencies and dates remain separate. Desktop and miniapp display server facts. Securities details expose period deposits, withdrawals, realized results, fees, cash and holdings.

Original observations, annotations and NAS evidence are not rewritten. Changed rule hashes invalidate the stored projection; the cloud builds a new durable result once. Synthetic checks cover immutable sources, restored materializations, loss/fee exclusion, balance classes, paging and compact miniapp responses. Live release evidence is retained separately under ignored output.
